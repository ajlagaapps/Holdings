# Privacy Policy for Holdings

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Holdings ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect and How It's Used

**Portfolio Data**
The App allows you to track investments you add manually. This portfolio data (e.g., symbols, quantities, cost basis, notes) is **stored locally on your device**. We do not upload, back up, or store your portfolio data on our servers.

**Market Data Requests**
To display current prices, the App sends the **market symbols** in your portfolio to a third-party market-data API. This request is limited to the symbol(s) needed to retrieve a price quote and does not include your name, holdings size, cost basis, or other personal details.

**Purchase and Subscription Information**
If you purchase a subscription or in-app product, purchase and entitlement information (such as subscription status and transaction details) is processed by:
- **RevenueCat**, our subscription management provider, and
- **Google Play**, which processes the payment transaction itself.

We do not directly receive or store your payment card details.

**Device Identifier for Trial Management**
The App may use a **pseudonymous device identifier** to manage free trial eligibility (for example, to prevent the same device from starting multiple trials). This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other directly identifying information we collect, because we do not collect that information (see below).

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App **does not require** you to provide:
- A name
- An email address
- A user account

You can use the App's core features without creating an account or providing personal identifying information.

## 3. Data Sharing and Sale

We **do not sell** your portfolio data.

We share limited data only as described above, with:
- The market-data API provider (market symbols only, to retrieve prices)
- RevenueCat and Google Play (purchase and entitlement information, and the device identifier used for trial management)

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent processors under their own privacy policies, linked below:
- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/
- Google Play / Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your portfolio data is stored locally on your device using standard device storage mechanisms. If you delete the App or clear its data, this information is removed from your device. We recommend keeping your device secure (e.g., using a passcode) since we do not maintain a server-side backup of your portfolio data.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Your Choices

Because the App does not require an account, there is no central account to delete. To remove your data, you can delete your portfolio entries within the App or uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored data from your device. To manage subscriptions, use your Google Play account subscription settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
